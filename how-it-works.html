How it works

From missed call to booked job.

Your phone number stays the same. Nothing new to learn.

Step by step

1

Your phone rings as usual

Nothing changes when you can answer. If you can't, the caller hears a short message: “Sorry we missed your call. Press 1 and we'll text you a link to book. Message and data rates may apply.”

2

They get a text in seconds

The text comes from a local number set up for your business and is signed with your business name, so they know exactly who it's from.

3

They pick a time. You approve it.

The booking link shows the times you're available. The request appears on your calendar with their name, number and address, and you get a notification. Approve it and they get a confirmation. Requests still waiting get a reminder. Prefer not to approve each one? Turn on auto‑confirm.

4

After the job, ask for a review

Send one short text with the customer's number and they get a polite Google review request. Every customer gets the same message.
