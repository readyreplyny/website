Frequently asked

Do I need a new phone number?

No. ReadyReply works with the business number you already have.

No. Texts go out automatically and bookings show up on your calendar.

If it's bringing you work, you move to one flat monthly rate between $79 and $149, agreed in writing. If not, I turn it off and you owe nothing.
